Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2021 as a small neighborhood shop. We wanted a local spot where people could grab fresh bread and coffee before work or pick up treats for their family.

How We Source Ingredients

We keep our recipes simple and buy ingredients from local farms when we can. We use unbleached flour, real butter, local honey, and fresh fruit with no artificial preservatives.
